\section{Lagrangian CMDP training for RL hosts}
\label{app:cmdp}

An unconstrained MDP chases \(\mathbb{E}[\sum_k \gamma^k r_k]\) and nothing else---no explicit budget on stalls or smoothness.
So when the base policy is learned, we may recast streaming as a constrained MDP (CMDP) that maximizes expected fidelity under budgets on trajectory-level auxiliary costs~\cite{altman1999constrained}.
Set \(c^{\mathrm{stall}}_k \equiv \tau^{\mathrm{stall}}_k\) and \(c^{\mathrm{sm}}_k \equiv \text{smooth}_k\).
The undiscounted episodic rebuffering ratio and mean smoothness cost read
\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{RB}(e)=\frac{1}{T^{\mathrm{play}}_{e}}\sum_{k\in\mathcal{K}_{e}} c^{\mathrm{stall}}_k,
    \qquad
    \mathrm{SM}(e)=\frac{1}{K_{e}}\sum_{k\in\mathcal{K}_{e}} c^{\mathrm{sm}}_k,
\end{equation}
with targets \(\delta_{\mathrm{rf}}{=}0.05\) and \(\delta_{\mathrm{sm}}{=}3.5\)~\cite{yin2015control}.
Per-step normalized costs \(\widetilde{\mathrm{RB}}_k\) and \(\widetilde{\mathrm{SM}}_k\) yield the Lagrangian-relaxed PPO reward
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:rtrain-app}
    r^{\mathrm{train}}_k
    = \frac{1}{100}\Big(
        q_k
        - \lambda_r \,\widetilde{\mathrm{RB}}_k
        - \lambda_s \,\widetilde{\mathrm{SM}}_k
        - w_b\,\phi(B_k)
        - w_\ell\,\psi^{\mathrm{Lya}}_k
    \Big),
\end{equation}
where projected ascent on the empirical constraint gaps updates the dual variables \((\lambda_r,\lambda_s)\)~\cite{paternain2019dual}.
Training may also subtract buffer-deviation and Lyapunov drift penalties~\cite{neely2010stochastic}; these steady the optimization but certify nothing.
The gap is real: finite-sample PPO does not certify per-chunk feasibility~\cite{schulman2017proximal,achiam2017constrained}, and CPS hands over that layer at deployment time.

The implementation rides Stable-Baselines~3 PPO~\cite{raffin2021sb3,schulman2017proximal}: two Tanh MLPs (width 256), GAE \((\gamma{=}0.99,\lambda_{\mathrm{GAE}}{=}0.95)\), dual step sizes \(\eta_r{=}0.012\) and \(\eta_s{=}0.003\), and projected ranges \(\lambda_r\in[4.3,40]\), \(\lambda_s\in[0.3,2.5]\) after a 60-episode warm-up.
As in the main text, co-design wraps the training environment in CPS; shield-at-eval-only policies train without it.
