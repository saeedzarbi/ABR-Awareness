On a monotone ladder, VMAF never decreases with the rung

The main contributions are:
• Certified Perceptual Shield. A model-agnostic runtime
projection that combines VMAF-knee bandwidth banking,
a conformal throughput lower bound with finite-sample cov-
erage under residual exchangeability, and certified buffer
feasibility.
• Isolated banking evaluation. Paired Raw, Safety, and
Certified rollouts on greedy, BBA, BOLA, RobustMPC,
and PPO hosts. We report Wilcoxon tests on bitrate and
rebuffering, and two one-sided tests (TOST) of VMAF
within ±ε [15].
• Optional predictive banking (supporting). A buffer- and
look-ahead-conditioned perceptual budget (CPS-P) that
widens to εrisk under risk. Section 5.3 compares it with
static ε sweeps; a larger static budget can cut rebuffering
further.
• Regime characterization.
Banking gains concentrate
where ladders saturate at the top and hosts request those
rungs (primary 5G suite). Real-trace broadband stress is
dominated by safety projection.
• Policy–shield co-design (supporting). An optional single-
seed PPO study with CPS in the training loop. We report
the QoE weight w⋆at which this co-trained policy crosses
shield-at-eval-only operation, but we do not treat the arm
as a headline claim.
To separate banking from safety, we run three paired arms
on identical episode seeds: Raw (unshielded), Safety (confor-
mal feasibility only), and Certified (feasibility plus banking).
The banking effect is the Certified minus Safety contrast. The
safety effect compares Safety or Certified against Raw. We
run 204 paired 5G episodes over twelve titles, with a percep-
tual budget ε=1.0 and a tolerated miss rate α=0.10. Certified
banking reduces mean bitrate and rebuffering on greedy, BBA,
BOLA, and RobustMPC (Table 5). For greedy, banking reduces
bitrate by 4.5% and rebuffering by 9.3%. Mean VMAF stays
within ±ε by TOST, and empirical coverage is 0.919 against
a 0.90 target. Optional CPS-P and static ε sweeps appear in
Section 5.3. On real fixed and mobile broadband traces from
the FCC Measuring Broadband America program and the Nor-
way/Nornet logs [16, 17], banking gains narrow to about 0.9%
bitrate reduction (certified vs. safety). Conformal feasibility still
reduces stalls by about 98.7% vs. raw greedy on those traces.
That host already stalls for most of the session, so the cut is a
feasibility effect. These results indicate where perceptual bank-
ing is effective. Optional PPO co-training with CPS remains a
supporting, single-seed study.
The remainder of this paper is organized as follows. Section 2
reviews related work, and Section 3 formalizes the streaming
model. Section 4 presents CPS and optional co-training. Sec-

Section 7 concludes.
